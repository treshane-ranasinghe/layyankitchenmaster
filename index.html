02Our story

About Layaan Kitchen Masters

Layaan Kitchen Masters designs, manufactures, supplies and installs commercial kitchen equipment and customised stainless-steel solutions — 12+ years in the industry, a client network that reaches across Sri Lanka and project experience in international markets.

Layaan Kitchen Masters has spent more than 12 years in the commercial kitchen industry, and that time has taught us what it takes to create kitchens that perform in demanding professional environments.

From a single piece of equipment to a complete kitchen project, our team brings together industry experience, technical knowledge, quality manufacturing and professional project coordination. Whether you are opening a restaurant, developing a hotel, expanding a bakery or establishing a large-scale food service operation, we provide the equipment and solutions you need.

Every commercial kitchen is different. We develop solutions around your space, workflow, production capacity, hygiene requirements and operational needs, so that your kitchen works efficiently from the first day of service and keeps working for years after it.
