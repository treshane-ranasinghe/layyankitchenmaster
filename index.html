02Our story

About KitchenGuard Pro

KitchenGuard Pro was founded in 2009 after our team spent years watching excellent kitchens fail routine inspections for avoidable reasons — an uncalibrated probe, a storage rack that was never food-grade, a sanitiser with no traceable batch record. Compliance-grade supply simply was not available locally without a long import wait, so we built the supply chain that closed that gap.

Today we combine two disciplines under one roof. Our food safety side is led by certified HACCP practitioners and former hotel QA managers who know exactly what an auditor opens first. Our supply chain side runs direct relationships with NSF and ISO 22000 certified manufacturers, so the documentation arrives with the pallet instead of six weeks later.

Our mission is simple: make every commercial kitchen we serve audit-ready on any given day, without warning. That means certified products, clear paperwork, honest lead times and a team that picks up the phone when a freezer log goes out of range at 11pm.
